# 🌊 WaveLength

WaveLength is an ocean-themed social music discovery platform that transforms the way people experience music. Instead of discovering songs through recommendation algorithms alone, WaveLength connects people through the music they love, creating a shared space where every song has a person behind it.

Using Spotify integration, users become part of a living, interactive ocean where songs float across the community. As users listen to music, discover new artists, and share their favourite tracks, their contributions become part of the wave for others to explore. Every floating song represents an opportunity to discover new music, meet like-minded people, and start meaningful conversations.

The goal of WaveLength is simple: **make music social again.**

---

# Project Vision

Music has always been one of the easiest ways to connect people, yet most streaming platforms focus on individual listening experiences. WaveLength extends Spotify by creating a community where music discovery naturally leads to conversation, collaboration, and friendship.

Instead of simply recommending songs, WaveLength allows users to discover the people behind those recommendations.

---

# How It Works

Users authenticate using their Spotify account and immediately become part of the WaveLength community.

Once logged in, they enter a shared ocean where songs contributed by other users float throughout the environment. As users continue listening to music, discovering new artists, and sharing songs, their corner of the ocean evolves with them.

Users can freely explore the ocean, selecting songs that catch their attention. Each song displays information about the track and the user who shared it, allowing visitors to discover music through real people rather than anonymous recommendation algorithms.

Every song provides options to:

- 🎧 Listen on Spotify
- ❤️ Like the song
- ➕ Save it directly to a Spotify playlist
- 👤 View the profile of the user who shared it
- 🤝 Follow users with similar musical tastes
- 💬 Send a chat request

To encourage meaningful interactions, users cannot message strangers directly. Instead, chat requests must first be accepted before a private conversation begins. Once connected, users can recommend music, discuss artists, and build friendships around shared musical interests.

Users can also create or join communities centred around genres, artists, moods, or interests, making it easy to meet people who enjoy the same kind of music.

# Weekly Community Challenges

Every week, WaveLength introduces a new community challenge designed to encourage users to discover and share music in fun and creative ways.

Challenges are intentionally non-competitive and focus on participation rather than winning.

Example challenges include:

- 🎵 Share a song from your childhood
- 🌍 Recommend a song in another language
- 💎 Share an underrated artist
- 🎬 Share your favourite movie soundtrack
- 🇿🇦 Recommend a South African artist
- 🔁 Share a song you've had on repeat this week

Instead of earning points, experience, or climbing leaderboards, users who participate receive temporary cosmetic profile enhancements such as glowing profile borders, animated ripple effects, or ocean-themed profile frames. (to be discussed)

The purpose of these challenges is simple: encourage music discovery, spark conversations, and strengthen the community without turning WaveLength into a gamified social network.

# Why WaveLength?

Most music platforms help users discover songs.

WaveLength helps users discover **people**.

By combining music discovery, social networking, and an immersive ocean-inspired interface, WaveLength creates a platform where every recommendation has a face behind it, every playlist has a story, and every conversation begins with a song.

Whether you're looking for your next favourite artist, people who share your taste in music, or simply a place where music feels social again, WaveLength offers a new way to explore, connect, and belong.
